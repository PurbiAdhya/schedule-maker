Set your preferences, then review an automatic draft. Typical instructors selected in Courses stay assigned; courses without one may be assigned to any instructor.

Credit-hour rule: Scheduled meeting time is 50 minutes per credit each week. Four-credit courses use MTRF when available. Laboratory courses keep one longer lab block.

Room preferences

A course rule overrides an instructor rule, which overrides a prefix rule. Prefer is flexible; Require must be satisfied for new placements.

Instructor preferences

Morning ends by noon; afternoon starts at noon. Back-to-back allows normal passing time (up to 20 minutes). Spaced leaves one full class slot between consecutive meetings on the same day. These are preferences, and any unmet preferences will be listed.
